About

Two fields that turned out to overlap more than expected

My background is in agriculture — a BSc from Sher-e-Bangla Agricultural University, followed by an MS in Genetics and Plant Breeding, where my thesis looks at variability and trait relationships across blackgram genotypes.

Alongside that, I've spent a lot of time teaching myself software development: Laravel and MySQL for full applications, React for interactive front ends, and enough Linux administration to run and deploy what I build. A lot of it started from wanting to solve a real problem I could see around me — students needing quick help with coursework, farmers needing a better way to reach buyers — rather than from a course syllabus.

I'm most interested in the places where the two overlap: using statistical and analytical thinking from genetics research to reason about software, and using development skills to make research and data easier to work with.

Research

Genetic variability in blackgram

Character association, correlation, and path coefficient analysis across Vigna mungo genotypes.

My MS thesis examines how yield-related traits vary and relate to one another across a set of blackgram genotypes, using correlation and path coefficient analysis to separate traits with a direct effect on yield from those that act indirectly through other characters. The aim is to identify which traits are the most useful selection criteria for future breeding work on the crop.

Crop — Blackgram (Vigna mungo) Methods — Variability, correlation, path coefficient analysis Supervisor — Dr. Nahid Zeba
